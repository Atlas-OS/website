This page explains which Atlas installations can update, and what an update keeps and changes.
Back up important files before you start.

## Check your Atlas version

Open Atlas Manager.
Home shows your installed version and, when Atlas Manager has a record of it, your installation history.

A release can update only from the versions it lists as `updatableFrom` in its [`atlas.json`](https://github.com/Atlas-OS/Atlas/blob/main/package/atlas.json).
If your version isn't listed, Atlas Manager says it can't update it directly.
To use the newer release, back up your files and reinstall Windows, then [install Atlas](/docs/install/).

If Atlas Manager says it **Couldn't read your Atlas installation details**, choose **Try again**.
If that doesn't help, [send a report](/docs/faq/report-a-problem/) before you update.

## Check your Windows release

Your Windows release must be one the new Atlas release supports.
See the [supported releases table](https://github.com/Atlas-OS/Atlas/blob/main/docs/windows-release-policy.md#supported-releases).

If your release is older, Atlas Manager moves Windows to a supported release before it updates Atlas.
It can also recommend a newer release when yours is still supported.
See [Move Windows to a newer release](/docs/upgrade/windows-release/).

## Check your Windows edition

Atlas Manager requires a supported Windows edition even if you already have Atlas installed.
If your PC runs Home, follow [Fix the Windows Home edition error](/docs/faq/windows-home/).
See [Windows edition requirements](/docs/install/requirements/#windows-edition).

## What an update keeps

- Your files and apps.
- Your Start menu and taskbar layouts, file associations and theme.
- Atlas's backups of your original services.
- Your Atlas choices, if you choose **Keep my choices**.
- Settings you changed in the Atlas folder.
  Atlas puts each recorded choice back after the update, using the new release's definitions.

Atlas can only recognise settings it recorded, or settings that clearly match one of its own choices.
Changes you made outside Atlas, such as your own registry edits, may not carry over.

## What an update changes

- Atlas replaces the Atlas folder and its modules.
  Atlas's own logs are kept, but anything else you saved in the Atlas folder is replaced, so move your own files elsewhere first.
- Atlas applies the new release's settings and fixes.
- If you choose **Choose again**, Atlas applies each answer as a new installation would.
  See [Keep my choices or Choose again](/docs/upgrade/upgrade/#3-keep-my-choices-or-choose-again).

An update doesn't repeat the app and component removals of a first installation.

## PCs with more than one account

Settings for the whole PC, such as services, policies and Windows Update, apply to every account.
Your own settings apply during the update.

Other existing accounts get their settings the next time each person signs in.
Some protected policies in those accounts may keep their older values.
Ask each person to sign in after the update and check their settings.

Atlas Manager updates Microsoft Store apps only for the account that runs it.
Apps that only another account has stay at their version until that person updates them in Microsoft Store under **Library** > **Get updates**.

<Callout type="info" title="Restarting with other people signed in">
  Before any restart, Atlas Manager checks whether someone else is signed in.
  It names them and restarts only if you choose **Restart anyway**, because restarting closes their apps and loses their unsaved work.
</Callout>
