When something goes wrong with Atlas Manager or Atlas, you can send a report privately to the Atlas team, or export a diagnostics ZIP to share when you ask for help.
This page explains both, and what the diagnostics contain.

<Callout type="tip" title="Collect diagnostics before you retry">
  Send a report or export diagnostics before you retry, clear Atlas Manager's data or reinstall Windows.
  Retrying keeps earlier logs, but reinstalling Windows loses them.
</Callout>

## Where to find the buttons

In Atlas Manager, open **Settings** and go to **Help and feedback**.
It has **Send a report** and **Export diagnostics**.

The same buttons appear under a problem, such as a failed check, installation, ISO or USB job.
**Report a problem** on Home opens **Send a report**.

## Send a report

**Send a report** sends your message privately to the Atlas team.

1. Answer **What would you like to send?** with **An issue** or **A suggestion**.
2. Under **Your message**, describe what you were doing, what you expected and what happened.
   Don't include passwords.
3. Optionally, add **Contact details (optional)**, such as an email address or Discord username.
4. Keep **Include diagnostics** on for an issue.
   Atlas Manager collects them as the page opens; choose **Review ZIP** to see the ZIP before you send it.
5. Select **I agree to send this report and any included diagnostics to the Atlas team**, then choose **Send report**.

When the report arrives, Atlas Manager shows **Report received** and a reference.
Keep the reference if you contact the Atlas team about the report.
If you left contact details, the team may reply, but a reply isn't guaranteed.

Reports go to the Atlas team at reports.atlasos.net and are deleted after 90 days.
The team may use AI services from other companies to help investigate; these get your message and diagnostics, but not your contact details.

If sending fails, your message stays on the page.
Choose **Try again**, or send it from the [report website](https://reports.atlasos.net), which shows the same privacy notice.

## Export diagnostics

**Export diagnostics** saves a diagnostics ZIP on your PC.
Use it when you report a bug on GitHub or ask for help in the community.
Those places are public, so review the ZIP before you share it.

1. Choose **Export diagnostics**.
2. When Atlas Manager shows **Diagnostics ZIP created**, choose **Show in folder** to find it.

ZIPs are saved in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AtlasOS\App\Diagnostics`.
Atlas Manager never deletes them, so remove them yourself when you no longer need them.
Nothing is uploaded unless you send a report.

### If Atlas Manager can't open

Open PowerShell in the folder that contains `AtlasManager.exe` and run:

```powershell
.\AtlasManager.exe --export-diagnostics
```

### Export from the right account

Export diagnostics from the account that had the problem.
Other people's accounts aren't collected.

## What the diagnostics contain

The ZIP contains Atlas Manager's logs, Atlas's installation logs, logs from updating Windows and creating media, Atlas Manager's settings and an installation report.
It doesn't contain programs, scripts, Windows images or memory dumps.

Atlas removes your user name, PC name, email addresses and known passwords or keys from the ZIP.
Error details, hardware models, app names and network addresses are kept, because they help find the cause.
The logs on your PC aren't changed.

Atlas can't recognise every kind of personal text, so review the ZIP before you share it publicly.

## What to include

Whichever way you report, say:

- what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead
- roughly when it happened, with your time zone
- whether it happens again when you retry
- your Atlas and Windows versions, and the choices you made
