# Network drivers

Wi-Fi and Ethernet adapters need network drivers to connect to the internet. If you followed the [Playbook guide](/docs/install/playbook/#step-2-prepare-network-drivers-recommended), use the driver installer saved on your USB drive.

## Finding your network adapter

1. Open **Device Manager** (press <CodeInline text="Win + X" /> <CodeInline text="Enter" /> or search in Start)
2. Expand **Network adapters**
3. Note the name of your Wi-Fi or Ethernet adapter (e.g., Intel Wi-Fi 6, Realtek PCIe GbE)

## Installing from manufacturer website

Download network drivers from your motherboard or device manufacturer's support page.

1. Search for "_your motherboard model_ support" or "_your laptop model_ drivers"
2. Find the **LAN** or **Wi-Fi** driver section
3. Download the latest driver for your adapter
4. Run the installer and follow the setup instructions
5. Restart your computer if prompted

<Callout type="info" title="Can't Find Drivers?">
  Search for your network adapter name (from Device Manager) plus "driver". Intel, Realtek, and
  Qualcomm provide generic drivers for their adapters.
</Callout>

## Common network adapter manufacturers

- **Intel** - [Intel Download Center](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/download/18231/intel-network-adapter-driver-for-microsoft-windows-11.html)
- **Realtek** - Search your motherboard manufacturer's support page (Realtek often doesn't offer direct downloads)
- **Qualcomm/Atheros** - Usually available from your laptop or motherboard manufacturer

## Troubleshooting

- **No internet after install:** Download network drivers before reinstalling Windows. If you have another device with internet, download there and transfer via USB.
- **Driver won't install:** Run the installer as administrator (right-click <CodeInline text="Run as administrator" />).
- **Still no connection:** Try [Snappy Driver Installer Origin](/docs/essential-setup/drivers/other/#snappy-driver-installer-origin-sdio) as a backup, select only your network adapter driver.
